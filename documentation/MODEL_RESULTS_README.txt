PASSASSIST NATIONAL — AI MODEL RESULTS

The evaluation file is generated by running:
python ai/train_models.py

The included 90-day, 10-office planning dataset is illustrative and is not official Ministry data. The model uses a chronological holdout: earlier dates for training and later dates for testing.

Targets:
- applications_received: planning estimate of application demand.
- outstanding_backlog: planning estimate of office workload.

Metrics:
- MAE: mean absolute error; lower is better.
- RMSE: root mean squared error; lower is better.
- R-squared: proportion of variance explained; closer to 1 is generally better. Negative values indicate performance worse than a constant-mean baseline.

The models support planning analysis only. They do not determine eligibility, approve applications, or replace an authorised officer.
