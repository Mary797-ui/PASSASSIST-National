ABOUT PASSASSIST NATIONAL

A simpler way to get prepared.

PASSASSIST National is a proposed digital platform designed to help Basotho citizens prepare for passport-related services before visiting a passport office.

It brings preparation guidance, document checklists and office information together. Its longer-term government dashboard vision is to help authorised officials understand application volumes, processing activity and passport-book inventory needs across regions.
